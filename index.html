Kalepa raises $75M Series B at a $470M valuation

New York-based Kalepa announced $75 million in Series B funding at a $470 million valuation, led by Updata Partners with participation from IA Ventures and Inspired Capital. Amdocs buys insurance core platform EIS for about $340M

Amdocs acquired San Francisco-based insurance core platform provider EIS for approximately $340 million in cash, with additional consideration possible based on performance metrics. The deal is Amdocs' first step into insurance; EIS offers a cloud-native, agentic multi-line platform spanning policy administration, billing, claims and customer engagement for life and annuity and P&C carriers including Aflac, CSAA, Allstate, Desjardins and BNP Paribas Cardif. Amdocs plans to extend aOS, its agentic operating system developed for telecommunications, into insurance to enhance the EIS platform.

InsurGrid launches AI policy-change monitoring for P&C agencies

San Francisco-based InsurGrid launched Policy Change Monitoring, which automatically refreshes connected client declaration pages and policy data every 15 days and alerts agencies when a policy is cancelled, reinstated, renewed, not renewed or materially changed. The platform serves more than 2,500 agents, connects to more than 450 carriers, has processed more than 500,000 declaration pages through AI extraction and has supported more than $250 million in closed premium. The feature is rolling out on InsurGrid's $99-per-month plan for supported carrier connections that do not require multi-factor authentication at each login.

Global insurtech funding fell 44.6% to $1.36B in Q3, Gallagher Re says

Gallagher Re's latest Global InsurTech Report found global insurtech funding fell 44.6% to $1.36 billion in the third quarter of 2026, largely because $100 million-plus mega-rounds dropped to $660 million from $1.67 billion in the prior quarter. Deal count stayed roughly steady at 104 transactions versus 107, while AI-focused companies attracted 83.2% of funding, or $1.13 billion across 72 deals. Early-stage funding rose 20.1% quarter on quarter to $317.3 million, and U.S.-based insurtechs accounted for a record 64.4% of deals.

SSP UK & Ireland acquires insurance software firm IS2

SSP UK and Ireland acquired insurance technology provider IS2, describing it as the firm's first major acquisition since becoming a standalone business; the deal size was not disclosed. Founded in 1996, IS2 provides policy administration and product distribution technology to insurers, insurtechs, MGAs and scheme brokers, including its Atlas platform and RAPID, an API-based channel that distributes scheme products through brokers, MGAs, software houses, digital banks, affinity partners and embedded insurance. Managing director Martyn Mathews said the purchase extends SSP into product distribution and 'will not be our last' as the firm looks at further deals.

Prudential launches AI Claims Insight Assistant for employer portals

Prudential Financial launched Claims Insight Assistant, an AI capability in its Benefit Center Employer Portal that generates one-click summaries of disability and absence claims, including status, recent activity and next steps. The tool reviews information already in Prudential's claims systems and does not make claim decisions, determine eligibility, or approve or deny claims; summaries include only information employers are already permitted to view. It is now available to all Group Insurance customers with access to disability and absence claims on the Benefit Center.

Open and IAG Loyalty add Avios rewards to UK car insurance

From 2 November 2026, British Airways Club members can collect Avios on car insurance purchased through Avios Insurance, which now sits alongside travel cover in a single account, with home insurance to follow. A launch offer through 27 November pays 20 Avios per £1 spent, double the standard rate of 10 Avios per £1, up to 10,000 Avios on a single policy. Open said the proposition, built on its AI-native platform for IAG Loyalty, went from plan to launch in under two months; Avios Insurance is a trading name of FCA-authorised Open Insurance Services UK Limited (FRN 988625).

Mentioned: Open, IAG Loyalty, Jason Wilby, Jack Smith
