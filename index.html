Data center cyber risk is running ahead of most policies

Novatae Risk Group’s Karen Kutger said data center cyber submissions are being underwritten against exposures that have outgrown standard policy wording, with dependent business interruption, MFA enforcement, encrypted backups, and AI-related coverage gaps now driving placement outcomes. The technical issue is that carriers are using external scans and submission documentation to test controls such as remote access, privileged access, EDR/MDR, and network-isolated backups, while many policies remain silent on AI output, deepfakes, pixel tracking, and biometric claims. This matters because control quality can change premium by up to 35%, carrier underwriting friction increases when exposures are not documented, and broker negotiations on dependent BI and explicit AI language affect capacity and claim response for data center accounts.
