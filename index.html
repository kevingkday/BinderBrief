Minnesota issues AI bulletin spelling out governance expectations for insurers

The Minnesota Department of Commerce issued Administrative Bulletin 2026-4, reminding all licensed insurers that decisions made or supported by AI systems must comply with insurance laws, including unfair trade practice and unfair discrimination rules. The department encourages insurers to maintain a written AI Systems (AIS) Program with controls scaled to the risk of adverse consumer outcomes, including testing for errors and bias and accounting for reliance on third-party data and models. It also sets out the kind of information and documentation the department may request when investigating or examining an insurer's AI use.

Gemini Notebook: turn your sources into interactive sites

Google's Gemini Notebook can now turn the sources in a notebook into interactive sites: type "make me a site" in the notebook chat with a description, such as an interactive dashboard to explore data or a project tracker for tasks and deadlines, then open it in a new tab or share it.
